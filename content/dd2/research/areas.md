# Areas in our own words (built from wiki location notes + quest data; items marked (confirm) are to verify during Rob's playthrough)
## Regions
Vermund: human kingdom ruled by a line of Sovran who earned the throne by defeating the Dragon; starting kingdom, west/central map.
Battahl: kingdom of the Beastren, a different culture and nation, reached by crossing the border; arid south-east.
Unmoored World: secret endgame region reached through a choice in Legacy; own NPCs, trials and post-game quests.
Norgan (DLC): snowy Far North region, separate map, own Portcrystals.
## Vermund places
Borderwatch Outpost: where you land after escaping the excavation site; has a Riftstone, shops, inn, lounge; first side quests (Provisioner's Plight, Ordeals of a New Recruit). Exit leads to northern Vermund and Mt. Alles base.
Melve: small burnt-out town southwest of the Outpost; inn, apothecary, houses, helpers needed; crafting materials on the edges.
Vernworth: the capital; Common, Merchant, Noble, Mercantile and Residential districts, Slums, Castle (gaol tower, vault, audience chamber), Gracious Hand, Pawn and Vocation Guilds. Hub for most main quests and ~25 side quests.
Harve Village: coastal village south of Trevo Mine, southwest of Vernworth; Northern Harve Ruins nearby hold bandits and a cell with loot.
Sacred Arbor: woodland settlement north of Vernworth around the Arborheart tree (Riftstone); woodcutters and craftsmen (Grisha, Glyndwr); Grisha's Armory.
Moonglow Garden: Beren's recruit-training area (Claw Them Into Shape).
Nameless Village: north of the Eastern Forest; a thief-run village (Srail) with an underground obstacle course and Depths; missable story quest.
Checkpoint Rest Town: border settlement between Vermund and Battahl; Celeste's Smithy, Ibrahim's Scrap Store, Myrddin's Home, Pawn/Vocation facilities; many shops.
Ancient Battleground: found by following the road out of Checkpoint Rest Town; ruined castle site with a Cyclops chest, locked rooms (Oskar's key quest).
Mountain Shrine: cave reachable only after speaking with the Sphinx; riddle site.
Moonglint Tower: special location open only during and after the endgame.
## Battahl places
Bakbattahl: capital of Battahl; Flamebearer Palace, Gaol, Pawn/Vocation guilds, Oxcart Station, Wanderer's Haven, Isaac's Wares, Brokkr's Smithy, Awaran's Arms; ropeway network.
Agamen Volcanic Island: volcanic region with Volcanic Island Camp (Armory, Riftstone, Geyser Hamlet hot spring), Drabnir's Grotto; hosts Magick Archer and Warfarer vocation quests.
Seafloor Shrine: submerged, opened after Convergence; armories (Vermundian, Battahli, Dwarven, Elven), guild branches.
Excavation Site: northern digsite with Overseers' Lodge, Agamen Ruins and apothecary (Zachary).
Flamebearer Palace: Beastren seat of power; audience chamber, prayer hall, Empress's chambers.
Wyrmsblood Forest, Indigent Bedlands, Misty Marshes, Malachite Forest, Windworn Gully: outdoor regions (see locations.json).
