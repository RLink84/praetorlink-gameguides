# DLC: Dark Arisen (tag every entry "DLC"). Released Oct 9, 2026 (PS5, Xbox Series, Steam; Switch 2 edition bundles base+DLC). Base game required.
Sources: Game8 DLC guide + weapon/armor lists (fetched 2026-11-15); Fextralife DLC page (pre-release info). Facts only; Game8 notes some tables are still work in progress ("TBD").
## Structure
- New region Norgan ("Far North"), snowy, not physically connected to base map; only Norgan Portcrystals work there. Hazards: blizzards, chilled status. ~15-20h story, 25h+ complete.
- 12 Lost Rites dungeons added to the BASE map (repeatable, unique weapon per vocation + upgrade materials): Vermund: Devilkin Hollow, Daemon's Corral, Sunken Spire, Interminable Corridor | Battahl: Mired Conduit, Embersand Mine, Shrouded Warren, Precipitous Catacombs, Wyrmsblood Arena | Unmoored World: Molten Gauntlet, Seafloor Sepulcher, Final Proving Ground
- Hard Mode: added free for all players alongside the DLC (more enemy damage/resistance, more XP and DCP).
- Gear system: "Spoils" (Norgan relics): unappraised drops, appraised at the settlement for randomized stats; Hal's workshop crafts some guaranteed pieces after Hammer and Tongs. Transmog (layered armor) works on Norgan armor only; unlocked by Reaching the Far Shore.
- New: corrupted enemies; new augments Advertence, Fortuity, Lupinity, Proficiency; new weapon skills for every vocation (from Obelisks in Norgan). No new vocations. Bonus: Explorer's Camping Kit sold in shops; original-game music/sound option (do not ship music in our app).
- How to start: Captain Brant at the Stardrop Inn, Vernworth, at night; pick storyline. "About the new Arisen..." = Norgan (recommended Lv 40); "About the uncovered ruins..." = Lost Rites (recommended Lv 20).

## Pass-4 DLC additions (Game8, 2026-11-15)
Portcrystals (Norgan): free portable one after Chilling Tidings (place only in Norgan, retrievable; base-game ones don't work). Fixed sites: Norgandian Settlement (middle of first base), Rivercave Campground (north of settlement outside the rivercave), Shrine of Wisdom (outside shrine ruins, far NW).
Obelisks of Benediction (9; Warfarer has none): give new weapon skills, stat gains, Spoils spawn boost for DCP; need an Offering Flame (from final chests of Lost Rites dungeons) to fully use. Mage: just east of settlement, need Climbing Hook (one in a house in Abandoned Village to its east). Fighter: NE of settlement on climbable hill with Beorcan party. Archer: NW of Valgr Ravine, high on mountain, approach from north. Thief: NE of Beorcan Stronghold, near main road, hostile. Sorcerer: south of Beorcan Stronghold (NW of settlement, across the lake). Warrior: inside Mad Lord's Chapel near Shrine of Wisdom Portcrystal; beat the Mad Lord miniboss. Mystic Spearhand: NE of Inner Valley Grotto, open after quest Sage's Wisdom: Riddle of the Body. Magick Archer: Norgan Snowfield near Snowslide Pass cave, ghouls guard. Trickster: west of marked spot via Climbing Hook, or through Gagana's Grotto east exit; Fimbl dragon guards.
Enemies: Beorcan (bandits with Direwolves, Valgr Ravine), Bugaboo (jumpy goblin-like, Snowfield), Straggler (pale undead-like Beorcan), Direwolf, Norgan Giant (club Cyclops-like), Snowfoot (white Ogre that steals, Snatching-Beast's Lairs), Blackdog (A Howl in the Dark), Winged Aethon (flying, harpy-like), Gagana (fire-breathing gryphon, The Castle in the Ice), Ghoul, Wisp (night), Nokken (Saurian variant), Fimbl (ice dragon; Riddle of the Soul), Lesser Demon (Lost Rites, Lorade), Barbatos (floating caster, Where There's a Will), Willan Barbatos, Blemmyae (eyeless Cyclops, Soul Searching), Vile Eye (Lost Rites). Superbosses: Evil Eye (Final Proving Ground), Empowered Fallen Dragon, Satanachia (Thane of Darkness, Nifhel Depth), Death returns. Slimes: blue/purple only killed by poison.
Corrupted enemies by Lost Rites: Devilkin Hollow: Corrupted Goblins | Shrouded Warren: Corrupted Hobgoblins, Asps, Succubi, Skeletons, Undead; bosses Corrupted Skeleton Lord, Dullahan, Lich, Cyclops | Precipitous Catacombs: Corrupted Hobgoblins, Succubi, Asps, Mages; bosses Corrupted Wight, 2 Golems, Drake | Wyrmsblood Arena: Oozes, Corrupted Harpy, Asps, Undead; bosses 2 Ogres, Garm, Wight, Cyclops, Chimera | Embersand Mine: Hobgoblins, Lava Slimes, Skeletons, Undead; bosses Corrupted Minotaur, Gorechimera | Daemon's Corral: Electric Saurians, Slimes, Minotaur, Asps
