# Main quests: summaries in our words (facts from Fextralife quest pages, 2026-11-15). Spoiler level 1 = premise, 3 = outcomes. Rewards where the wiki lists them.
1 A Gaoled Awakening: prologue; you escape the excavation site (a Gorgon fight) and are shot from the sky. [page empty on wiki; details TODO]
2 Tale's Beginning: you wake at a waterfall; your companion Rook is swallowed by the Brine; soldier Justinn takes you to safety. No branching.
3 In Dragon's Wake: you recover at Borderwatch Outpost; news of an attack on Melve sends you toward the stronghold seeking your lost memories. Has multiple endings depending on whether you fully accompany Gregor. Opens many side quests (see side_quests.json).
4 Seat of the Sovran: at Melve your memory returns; Watchhead Gregor escorts you to the capital, where you are held until Captain Brant helps prove your identity. Opens Vernworth side content (Jadeite Orb, Dulled Steel, Oxcart Courier etc.).
5 Monster Culling: Brant assigns three jobs: goblins at Trevo Mine, saurians at Harve Village, a delivery at Vermund's eastern edge.
6 Disa's Plot: Brant wants proof of the queen's misdeeds; infiltrate the castle via the rear gate between midnight and dawn. Time-dependent, no branching.
7 The Caged Magistrate: free the imprisoned magistrate Waldhar from the gaol tower. Rewards 7,000G, 1,200xp, Ferrystone. Waldhar choice affects Heel of History tie-in.
8 The Stolen Throne: infiltrate a royal masquerade to investigate a false Sovran. Rewards 900xp, Wakestone Shard, 6,500G. Gives Myrmecoleon Card.
9 An Unsettling Encounter: sneak into Allard's Chambers in the Royal Quarter after Sven reports a suspicious delivery.
10 Feast of Deception: you attend the coronation but the Godsway's power lets the false Sovran override you. Side quests cancel here: Readvent of Calamity.
11 Nation of the Lambent Flame: travel to Battahl seeking answers about the Godsway; meet Menella. Opens Battahl quests.
12 Flickering Shadows: investigate researchers using blue crystals to power the Godsway; discover your link to a place under the sea (Olta'Battahl coastline shards).
13 Convergence: a structure rises from the seabed near Harve (Seafloor Shrine); you explore it after hearing the Rivage Elder's story. Gives Dulled Godsbane Blade.
14 A New Godsway: collect items to fix the blade (Forbidden Magick Research Lab, Wyrmsblood Forest; Ambrosius, Sigurd, Marthe). Rewards 35,000G, 6,000xp, Wakestone, Empowered Godsbane Blade. LAST SAFE POINT for many side quests (PowerPyx).
15 The Guardian Gigantus: the colossus Talos marches on Moonglint Tower; unfolds with time, outcome shaped by earlier choices (e.g. Raghnall). Reward 19,000xp. Side quests largely close after.
16 Legacy: confront the Dragon at Moonglint Tower; your highest-affinity companion is taken hostage; choose to flee or fight.
## Endings (Fextralife, updated Oct 2026)
Base: Bad = flee from the Dragon and confirm Yes (somber coronation, credits). Good = fight and defeat the Dragon (coronation and banquet; sit on throne for trophy "Peace"). True = need Empowered Godsbane Blade; talk to the Pathfinder instead of taking the throne to retry the fight, ride the Dragon, reach its chest/heart, use the blade; leads to Unmoored World. Not uncovering the truth unlocks New Game Plus.
DLC (Norgan): Normal = spare Ser Rovall's remains in The Castle in the Ice, then Reunion and beat the Fallen Dragon. Bad = destroy the remains, fight Eir, game over (reload). True = after Reunion, do Into Rancor and defeat Satanachia in Nifhel Depth. Bad 2 = die to Satanachia.
