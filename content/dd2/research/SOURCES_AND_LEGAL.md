## Capcom
Video policy (2021): can't pose as official, no pre-release spoilers, no cheat tools. Fan creation guidelines (Nov 2025): must state it is unofficial/based on Capcom IP, nothing mistakable for official, no direct copying of Capcom art without meaningful change, commercial use mostly restricted, Capcom may demand takedown. Primary pages not read in full: verify before launch. Not monetizing helps but is not a safe harbor.
## Stores (from general knowledge, verify)
Apple 5.2 (IP) and Google Play IP/impersonation policies: no Capcom name/logo/art in icon or title as if official; app name like "Unofficial Guide for ..." with disclaimer; apps with ads/free are fine. Risk: stores can still remove on rights-holder complaint. Web/PWA is a lower-risk first release.
